# Page Audit Checklist

Before finalizing any page:

## Layout
- Does each section have a unique identity?
- Is the page too template-like?
- Is there enough composition contrast?

## Spacing
- Are sections breathing?
- Are headings, body, and CTAs grouped correctly?
- Is whitespace being used as a luxury signal?

## Visual Quality
- Are there flat areas that need depth?
- Are images large enough?
- Are cards too generic?
- Are backgrounds too plain?

## Brand Feel
- Does it feel like a premium materials company?
- Does it feel architectural and global?
- Would a high-end client trust this brand?

## UX
- Is the flow clear?
- Are conversion points visible?
- Is the scroll journey intentional?

## Mobile
- Does the premium feel survive on smaller screens?
- Is the hierarchy preserved?
- Are CTAs touch-friendly?
