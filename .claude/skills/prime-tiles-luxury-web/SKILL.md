## PAGE AUDIT WORKFLOW (MANDATORY)

Before editing or generating code, internally perform this audit:

### 1. Layout Audit
Check:
- Is the layout too generic?
- Are sections too similar?
- Is there enough asymmetry or composition variation?
- Are there clear focal points?
- Does the page feel premium or template-like?

### 2. Spacing Audit
Check:
- Are sections cramped?
- Is vertical rhythm weak?
- Are margins inconsistent?
- Is text too close to images?
- Does the page breathe?

### 3. Hierarchy Audit
Check:
- Is the heading scale strong enough?
- Are subheadings clear?
- Are CTAs visible?
- Are benefits/scannable content structured well?
- Does the eye know where to go first?

### 4. Visual Depth Audit
Check:
- Are sections too flat?
- Are there premium shadows, borders, overlays, separators?
- Do images feel immersive?
- Is there enough material-inspired richness?

### 5. Product Presentation Audit
Check:
- Are products the hero?
- Do images feel large and premium?
- Is there emotional product storytelling?
- Do product areas feel showroom-quality?

### 6. Motion Audit
Check:
- Are hover states premium?
- Is there reveal-on-scroll?
- Are interactions subtle and elegant?
- Does motion improve experience instead of distracting?

If any of the above is weak, redesign.

For detailed review steps, see [PAGE_AUDIT_CHECKLIST.md](./PAGE_AUDIT_CHECKLIST.md)

## MOTION & INTERACTION RULES

Use motion only to increase perceived quality.

- reveal on scroll
- fade + translate entrances
- staggered card reveals
- image zoom on hover
- button hover transitions
- underline / border animations
- subtle parallax when appropriate
- smooth micro-interactions

Rules:
- motion must be subtle
- motion must feel expensive
- motion must be performance-friendly
- motion must never be flashy
- motion must support the content
